# Rune Mage Tower Defense

Tower Defense meets Tetris in this rune-building strategy game.

Defend your way through 18 increasingly difficult levels as you escape the Unicorn Archipelago!

Prevent enemies from reaching you (the rune mage) by inscribing Runes (aka Towers) onto the map. Survive all waves of enemies in a level to pass. Every time an enemy reaches you, you'll lose 1 health and banish the enemy back to the start of the map. You lose if your health drops below 0.

If you've never played a tower defense game before, check out the Tips section below.

## Controls
Select which type of rune (aka tower) to inscribe via the buttons in the sidebar.

Click the map to inscribe a rune. Runes block enemy movement, so you may not inscribe a rune that would block enemies from reaching yourself.

Every rune costs a certain amount of mana (ᚯ). The more times you've inscribed a certain rune, the more that rune will cost.

The three basic runes are:
* Red: Deals AoE damage to all enemies in range, but infrequently.
* Green: Deals damage to one enemy in range.
* Blue: Deals NO damage, but the cheapest to build.

Inscribing the same rune atop itself will create an upgraded version (up to a max of level 3).

Adjacent runes can combine to form more advanced types of towers. There are many different types of towers to discover! Try experimenting by placing runes next to each other.

Hovering a rune, tower, or enemy will reveal more details about it.

Gain mana by killing enemies. You also gain mana slowly over time. Banished enemies (those that reached you and were returned to the start of the map) DO NOT reward any mana.

## Keyboard Controls
You can also select which type of rune to inscribe via the keyboard.
* [1] - Red
* [2] - Green
* [3] - Blue

You can pause or speed up time via the keyboard.
* [space] - pause/resume
* [q] - switch to 3x speed

## Mechanic Cheat Sheet
* Fire - deals damage to enemies over time. Does NOT stack: only the strongest fire effect on an enemy applies.
* Slow - decreases enemy speed. Does NOT stack: only the strongest slow effect on an enemy applies.
* Poison - deals damage to enemies over time. DOES stack. More stacks of poison deal damage more quickly.
* Armor - Each point of armor on an enemy blocks 1hp of damage per hit. (eg, a bolt of 3 damage on an enemy with 1 armor will deal 2 total damage.) Armor does not prevent fire nor poison.

## Tips
Try to maximize the time enemies spend in range of your towers. Block shortcuts and place towers near curves and switchbacks.

Different types of towers are good against different enemies (ex, lightning is good against many small enemies but bad against large armored enemies). Pay attention to the types of waves in a level, and experiment with different tower types.

If the game is too difficult, try to discover more types of towers. Check out the Runebook to see which shapes form valid towers.

If the game is too easy, try to pass the levels without losing any health. Every level (yes, even that one) can be perfected with the right combination of towers.
