# Rune Mage Tower Defense
Tower Defense meets Tetris in this rune-building strategy game. Defend your way through 18 levels as you escape the Unicorn Archipelago!

## How to Play
Prevent enemies from reaching you (the Rune Mage) by inscribing Runes (aka Towers) onto the map. Survive all waves of enemies in a level to advance. Every time an enemy reaches you, you lose 1 health and **banish** the enemy back to the start of the map.

## Controls
Select which type of **rune** (aka tower) to inscribe via the buttons in the sidebar, or with <kbd>1</kbd>/<kbd>2</kbd>/<kbd>3</kbd>.

Every rune costs a certain amount of **mana (ᚯ)**.

Click the map to inscribe a rune. Runes block enemy movement, so you may not inscribe a rune that would block enemies from reaching yourself.

The three color runes are:
* Red: Deals damage to ALL enemies in range, but infrequently.
* Green: Deals damage to one enemy in range.
* Blue: Deals NO damage, but the cheapest to build.

Inscribing the **same rune atop itself** will create an upgraded version (up to a max of level 3).

Adjacent runes can **combine** to form more advanced towers. There are many different towers to discover! Try experimenting by placing runes next to each other.

Gain mana by killing enemies. You also gain mana slowly over time.

You can pause or speed up time via the keyboard.
* <kbd>space</kbd> - pause/resume
* <kbd>q</kbd> - 3x speed

## Mechanic Cheat Sheet
* Fire - deals damage to enemies over time. Does NOT stack.
* Slow - decreases enemy speed. Does NOT stack.
* Poison - deals damage to enemies over time. Stacks.
* Armor - Each point of armor on an enemy blocks 1hp of damage per hit. (eg, a bolt of 3 damage on an enemy with 1 armor will deal 2 total damage.) Armor does not block fire nor poison.

## Tips
If the game is too difficult, try to discover more types of towers. Check out the Runebook to see which shapes form valid towers.

If the game is too easy, try to win without losing any health. Every level can be perfected with the right combination of towers.
